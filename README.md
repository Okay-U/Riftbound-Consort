# Riftcount

Companion app for the Riftbound trading card game. Published as "Riftcount: Score Tracker"
by Pitopia. iOS ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755601459)) and Android from one repo.

Scoreboard (2 or 4 players, conquer/hold, XP, timer, undo), dice, card catalogue, deck builder
with import/export, draw odds and game history, and an Events tab for organized play.
No ads, no analytics, no accounts of our own. Privacy policy: https://okay-u.github.io/privacy.html

- iOS: `Riftbound Companiokay.xcodeproj`, SwiftUI, zero dependencies. Build in Xcode.
- Android: `android/`, a [Skip](https://skip.dev) Fuse app. `skip android build`, `skip export`.
- Conventions and working rules: `CLAUDE.md`. Plans and audits: `docs/`.
