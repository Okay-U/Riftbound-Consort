Riftbound AMBER Ledger

Every costed Riftbound card scored against the designers' recovered cost ledger. AMBER is value divided by printed cost, with 1.00 as par. It measures the numeric budget a card got, not how strong it plays. Method and prices from Chen & Lee (2026), frozen model v6 (CC BY 4.0).

What the ledger says about the designers
  • One ledger for everything. Median AMBER is 1.00 in every set, domain and rarity. Riot prices from a fixed exchange table, not card by card.
  • Fixed exchange rates. One rune icon is two energy. A point of damage or might is one energy. A card draw is two. An unconditional kill is eight. A point is 11.5.
  • Speed is free. Action, Reaction and Hidden add no cost. Riftbound uses them as domain identity, not as a priced upgrade.
  • Half for maybe. Repeatable triggers, Deathknell and on-play combat tricks are charged at about half. One-off "if… then" bonuses are mostly charged nothing.
  • Cheap cards run hot. Cost moves in whole energy, so small extras on 1–3 C cards are rounded away as gifts. Expensive cards sit slightly below par.
How the score is calculated
  • Cost. C = energy + 2 × power. One rune icon is worth two energy in the designers' ledger.
  • Base value V0. Might plus every priced effect, with conditions unmet and repeatable triggers fired zero times. Damage and might cost 1 C per point, a card draw 2 C, an unconditional kill 8 C, a point 11.5 C. Action, Reaction and Hidden are free.
  • Discounts. Assault and Shield cost 0.5 per point. Combat tricks on a unit's play effect count half. On a 2–3 C unit the play effect counts 30%. Deathknell counts half. One-off "if… then" bonuses count zero.
  • AMBER = (V0 + k × Δ) / C. Δ is the value of one firing of a unit's repeatable trigger. k is 0.5, the designers' "half an activation", and 0.25 when the trigger needs a further condition. Spells and gear have k = 0.
  • Gear. Reusable gear is valued over the designers' expected number of uses: three if it enters ready, two if it enters exhausted or needs a trigger, one if Temporary. That count is a judgement call the paper does not test, so gear scores are rougher than unit and spell scores. Seals score 3.00 for this reason.
  • Bands. Under curve < 0.86, on curve 0.86–1.17, above curve > 1.17, far above ≥ 1.50. The band is the paper's empirical normal range.
  • Status. Full means every effect is priced. Partial is a lower bound because an effect has no price yet. Deferred cards are not scored because a rule excludes them, for example counters, effects on all units or damage equal to might.
  • Sources. "Authors" cards use the authors' published per-card value, with their note translated. "Blind" cards (Radiance and cards the authors set aside) were priced by us with the same frozen rules from rules text only, with costs hidden.
